Method

      
Four passes, one photograph.

    

    

      
Pass 01
Read

        
The photograph is cut into regions — every surface and object separated from its neighbours.

      
Pass 02
Name

        
Each region is identified. A wardrobe, a doorway, a pile of laundry: each named for what it actually is.

      
Pass 03
Hold

        
Structure and circulation are locked. The model is given a boundary it cannot paint across.

      
Pass 04
Draw

        
Everything unheld is redrawn in the chosen style. Several options at once, from the one reading.

    

  


  


  
  

    

      
The rule

      
What a renovation is allowed to touch.

    

    
Every region falls on one side of a line. The line is the product.

    

      

        
RegionTreatmentWhy

        
          
DoorsHeldThe opening is where the building says it is.

          
WindowsHeldDaylight and sightlines are fixed facts.

          
WalkwaysHeldA design that blocks the route is not a design.

          
FurnitureOpenThe reason anyone is here.

          
Walls & floorOpenPaint, paper, timber, tile — the largest lever in the room.

          
ClutterOpenLaundry, bags and bottles are cleared, not reproduced.

Where it sits

        
It does not replace the designer. It gets the client to the conversation faster.

      

      

        
A client sends a photograph of the room they already live in and sees it three
          ways before the first meeting. The awkward part of the brief — what could this
          even look like — is answered up front, in their own room, at their own proportions.

        
What comes back is not a mood board. It is that room, with its door and its window
          and its walkway intact, furnished differently.
