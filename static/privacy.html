Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

Second Draft redesigns a room from a photograph. This page says what we keep, who sees it, and how to remove it.

What we collect

  • Your photos and the designs drawn from them. Photos you upload and the redesigns we create.
  • Your requests. The style, room type and notes you type, and any chat messages in the studio.
  • Account details, if you sign in. Name, email, account type (customer or firm), company name for firms, and either a password (stored only as a one-way hash) or your Google account ID.
  • Usage counts. How many designs a visitor or account has made today, so we can apply daily limits.
  • Feedback you choose to send.

Signing in with Google

If you use Google sign-in we receive your name, email address and Google account ID, and nothing else. We do not read your Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendar.

Who processes your photos

To read and redraw a room, your photos and requests are sent to an AI provider (OpenAI) and returned to us. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Sharing with a design firm

Your designs go to a design or fit-out firm only if you choose to send them, for example with the WhatsApp button. Nothing is shared with a firm automatically.

Cookies

We use a sign-in cookie to keep you signed in, and a short-lived cookie while you sign in with Google. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Keeping and deleting

  • Signed-in users can delete any design or whole conversation from their history; deleting removes it from our database.
  • To delete your account and everything under it, message us using the contact on the site and we will remove it.

Security

Passwords are hashed, connections use HTTPS, and secrets are kept out of the code. No online service is perfectly secure, so please do not upload photos that show documents, faces or anything you would not want stored.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: use the contact (WhatsApp) shown on the site.
